# CLBI Benchmark Evaluation Metrics Documentation

**Project:** Candidate Line Breach Intelligence (CLBI)  
**Document Version:** v1.0  
**Evaluation Protocol:** 5-fold Leave-One-Fire-Profile-Out (LOFO) Cross-Validation  

> **Evidence boundary:** The CLBI inputs and labels are generated by
> `src/data/build_dataset.py`. Fire names identify synthetic parameter profiles, not
> observed incident data. The label is derived from a formula that uses several model
> predictors. Results below describe this synthetic benchmark only; they are not
> measurements of real-world predictive accuracy, calibration, or inspection yield.

---

## Metric Definitions & Benchmark Results

### 1. Precision-Recall AUC (PR-AUC)
- **Definition:** Area under the Precision-Recall curve. Primary metric for the generated target (approximately 29.9% positive prevalence).
- **Why It Matters:** Evaluates how effectively the model balances precision and recall without being inflated by true negatives.
- **Evaluation Protocol:** 5-fold leave-one-generated-profile-out.
- **Reported Result:** **0.9507 ± 0.0080** (Exp C; mean ± standard deviation across folds)

---

### 2. Brier Score
- **Definition:** Mean squared error between model score $P(\text{Breach})$ and generated binary target $Y \in \{0, 1\}$.
- **Why It Matters:** Evaluates score accuracy against the synthetic target only; it does not establish calibration on observed outcomes.
- **Evaluation Protocol:** Mean of fold Brier scores across generated profiles.
- **Reported Result:** **0.1207** (Exp C)

---

### 3. Expected Calibration Error (ECE)
- **Definition:** Sample-weighted absolute difference between mean model score and generated positive-label rate across equal-width bins ($\text{ECE} = \sum \frac{n_b}{N} |\text{acc}_b - \text{conf}_b|$).
- **Why It Matters:** Quantifies score agreement with synthetic labels only, not real-world probability calibration.
- **Evaluation Protocol:** 10 equal-width bins and 20 equal-width bins over 4,600 generated held-out predictions.
- **Reported Result:** **0.0109 (10 Bins)** | **0.0151 (20 Bins)**

---

### 4. Top-10% Inspection Precision
- **Definition:** Percentage of generated positive labels among the top 10% highest-scoring generated segments.
- **Why It Matters:** Demonstrates ranking calculations on the synthetic benchmark; it is not an estimate of field inspection yield.
- **Evaluation Protocol:** Evaluated per held-out generated profile and averaged across 5 folds.
- **Reported Result:** **89.6% Mean** (Range: 84.0% to 95.8%)

---

### 5. Top-20% Breach Recall
- **Definition:** Percentage of generated positive labels captured within the top 20% highest-scoring generated segments.
- **Why It Matters:** Demonstrates ranking coverage on the synthetic benchmark; it is not a real-world resource-allocation result.
- **Evaluation Protocol:** Evaluated per held-out generated profile and averaged across 5 folds.
- **Reported Result:** **53.8% Mean** (Range: 48.8% to 57.2%)

---

### 6. Offline Breach-Yield Enrichment Ratio
- **Definition:** Ratio of generated positive labels captured per 10 segments using model top-10% selection versus random selection ($8.74 / 2.99$).
- **Why It Matters:** Compares selection rules on generated rows; it does not estimate field prioritization yield.
- **Evaluation Protocol:** 100-segment synthetic offline simulation.
- **Reported Result:** **2.92× versus Random Selection**
