A physics test

The data has no map orientation, so we let the wind decide

Fire should spread downwind. We tested all four row/column conventions on 14,979 patches.

Only rows south→north gives a real effect: 1.12× (95% CI 1.09–1.15).

The usual assumption gives 1.01×: no wind effect at all.

Presenter: Pratik. Why it matters: every wind and slope feature depends on knowing which way is north. Confidence intervals come from resampling patches. The effect is modest because wind is a daily ~4 km average. Terrain: sloped ground near fire burns about 2× more than level ground.
