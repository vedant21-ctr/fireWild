Limitations and next steps

What limits us, and what Phase 2 must prove

Limitations

  • Noisy daily MODIS labels: clouds, overpass timing
  • No fuel moisture, fuel type, suppression or hourly wind
  • Audit catches exact 8 × 8 km matches only
  • Spatial test holds smaller fires than train

Phase 2 plan

  • U-Net on the 12-channel patch, same splits and metrics
  • ConvLSTM only with multi-day data (WildfireSpreadTS)
  • Hybrid: engineered channels or LightGBM output into the U-Net
  • Must beat XGBoost on the spatial test, paired test
